GERMAN STEELS — ADMIN DELETE AND REMOVE CHANGES
Release date: 29 October 2026
For the testing team

IMPORTANT BEFORE TESTING
The backend team said the new removal APIs are not deployed yet. The new actions
will work on the test/live site only after those APIs are deployed there.
Use an Admin account and test records. Do not remove real business records.

HOW THE NEW REMOVE BUTTONS WORK
1. Open the page and click its remove, cancel, or void button.
2. A confirmation box opens and shows the record being removed.
3. Enter a reason. A reason is required and can be up to 500 characters.
4. Click the final confirm button once.
5. The list refreshes after success. The original record is kept for history.
6. Click Keep record or close the box to leave everything unchanged.
7. If the server blocks the action, the reason is shown in the box.

NEW ACTIONS TO CHECK

1. VISITS
Go to Visits and click the cancel icon beside a visit.
You can also open Visit Details and click Cancel visit at the top.
Expected: the visit leaves the active list. Linked tasks are cancelled.
If active expenses are linked to the visit, archive those expenses first.
The backend updates the daily report for a completed visit.

2. PRICING
Go to Pricing and click the remove icon beside a recorded price.
Expected: the entry disappears from the active list and price summary refreshes.

3. NOTES
Open a Retail Customer, Institution, Project, or Visit and open Notes.
Click the remove icon beside the note and enter a reason.
Expected: the note disappears from the normal list. Its history is kept.

4. PROJECT SALES
Open Projects, choose a project, and open Sales.
Click the Void sale icon beside the sale and enter a reason.
Expected: the sale leaves the active list and the project sales total refreshes.
The backend also excludes it from target achievement totals.

5. ATTACHMENTS
Visit Details: click Manage attachments at the top.
Documents: preview a document, then click Manage attachments.
Institution or Project Details: open Documents, then Manage attachments.
Notes: click Manage attachments beside the note.
Complaints or Requirements: open View images and click the remove icon.
Expenses: open the receipts window and click the remove icon under a receipt.
Expected: only the selected file is removed from the active files.
The server keeps the stored file for history.
A file used as accepted approval evidence cannot be removed.
Removing the last file from a document version also makes that version inactive.
Removing a receipt does not remove the expense claim itself.

6. RETAIL ACCOUNT BRANDS
Open Retail Customers, choose a customer, and open Relationship.
Under Active brands, click the remove icon beside a brand.
You can also open a retail Visit, open Brands, and use Retail account brands.
Expected: the brand leaves the whole customer's active brand list.
IMPORTANT: this affects the whole customer account, not only the current visit.
The old visit-only brand/pros-and-cons delete button has been removed because it
did not save a deletion to the server. Visit-only pros and cons stay in history.

7. MEETING ATTENDEES, GIFTS, AND EXPENSES
Open Meetings and choose a meeting. Click Manage meeting items.
Save or discard any unfinished meeting edits first.
The window lists Attendees, Expenses, and Gifts, including inactive items.
Click the remove icon beside one item and enter a reason.
Expected: the item becomes Inactive in history and the meeting totals refresh.
Remove active gifts linked to an attendee before removing that attendee.
Saved items in the meeting edit screens also use the new confirmation box.
Unsaved rows can still be removed locally before saving.

8. REMOVAL HISTORY
Click Removal history near the top of a dashboard page.
Choose a record type, enter its ID, and click Check history.
Or choose a record under Recently removed in this browser.
Expected: the history shows the reason, employee ID, and removal time.
An ID without history shows a clear empty message.
The browser's recent list is a shortcut; server history can be checked by ID.
The meeting item window also has history buttons for individual items.
This new history view covers the new removal actions above, not all older
archive actions in the application.

PREVIOUSLY ADDED CONTROLS TO RECHECK
- Retail Customers and Customer Groups: archive/deactivate with confirmation.
- Institutions and Projects: archive from the list or the detail-page menu.
- Employees: archive from the three-dot menu. History stays; login is disabled.
- Expenses: archive from card or table view. Reimbursed claims are protected.
- Documents: archive a document. Accepted approval evidence is protected.
- Compliance Issues: archive closed issues only.
- Attendance Correction: cancel pending requests only.
- Attendance: open a calendar day and use Reset attendance.
  Approved attendance corrections must use the correction process instead.
- Complaints and Requirements: cancel open items with confirmation.
  Completed items cannot be cancelled.
- Sales Regions: deactivate regions, PIN mappings, and districts.
- Settings > Teams: confirm before removing a member or deactivating a team.
- Settings > Working Days: confirm before making a rule inactive.
- Settings > Targets: confirm before deactivating or clearing saved targets.
- Meeting Details: cancel a meeting with confirmation; closed meetings are protected.
- Retail Customer and Institution Sales: the existing sale-void actions remain.

COMMON TEST CHECKS
- Keep record, Escape, and the close button must not remove anything.
- Empty or spaces-only reasons must not be accepted.
- The reason field must stop at 500 characters.
- Repeated clicks must not send duplicate removal requests.
- Check the item is gone from active lists after refreshing the page.
- Check the related count or total updates where applicable.
- Check removal history shows the correct record and reason.
- A Manager account must not see the new admin-only removal controls.
- Check the confirmation box on both desktop and mobile.
- If an endpoint is not deployed, an error must appear; the item must not be
  removed only from the screen or falsely reported as deleted.

REMAINING SEPARATE ITEMS
Meeting configuration (meeting types, gift items, expense heads) and the old
Enquiries page still need a confirmed backend contract. They are not new delete
features in this release. Do not treat them as completed removal features.

TESTING DONE BEFORE HANDOFF
Requests were mocked for testing. No production records were deleted.
Live testing of the new APIs remains pending backend deployment.
